## 2. Tier A facts — cited and marked
### E03.01.02 Ovulation (A, core) · AN77.2
- Each cycle, FSH rescues **15–20** primary (preantral) follicles from dying; usually only **one** reaches full maturity and releases its oocyte, the rest become atretic. `[E03.01]` **✔** (link Ch 2 §3f)
- Just before ovulation the follicle swells to ~**25 mm** (mature vesicular, graafian). `[E03.01]` **✔**
- Oestrogen from the follicle triggers the **LH surge**, which makes the oocyte finish **meiosis I** and start meiosis II, stops it in **metaphase II ~3 h before ovulation**, turns on progesterone making (luteinization) and makes the follicle burst. `[E03.01]` **✔**
- At the bulge an avascular spot (**stigma**) appears; LH raises collagenase (digests the follicle wall) and prostaglandins (wall muscle contracts) → the oocyte, with cumulus cells around it, is pushed out. `[E03.01]` **✔**
- Cumulus cells rearrange round the zona pellucida as the **corona radiata**. `[E03.01]` **✔**
- The book places ovulation at **midcycle** of a ~28-day cycle. `[E03.01 CC, E03.06 · R05ox, R05oy]` **✔✔** on "midcycle, on average" — but *human* data show the day moves: in a prospective study of 696 cycles only **~30 %** of women had their whole fertile window inside days 10–17 (R05ox); in 612,613 app-tracked ovulatory cycles the mean cycle was **29.3 days**, the follicular phase **16.9 days** (95 % range 10–30) and the luteal phase **12.4 days** (7–17) (R05oy) — refutes the map misconception "ovulation is always on day 14".

### E03.02.02 Fertilization (A, core) · AN77.4
- Fertilization happens in the **ampulla** of the uterine tube — its widest part, close to the ovary. `[E03.02]` **✔** — refutes the map misconception "fertilization happens in the uterus".
- Sperm may stay alive in the female tract for **several days**; the egg dies **~24 h** after ovulation if not fertilized. `[E03.02 · R05aq]` **✔✔** (*human*, 221 women: conceptions came only from intercourse in a **6-day window ending on ovulation day**)
- Only ~1 % of deposited sperm enter the cervix; they get to the tube mostly by contractions of the uterus and tube, in 30 minutes to 6 days; ~300–500 of 200–300 million reach the ampulla, and one fertilizes. `[E03.02]` **✔**
- Sperm wait in the isthmus, then become active again at ovulation (perhaps drawn by cumulus chemicals) and swim to the ampulla. `[E03.02]` **✔**
- Three barriers in order: **(1) corona radiata** → **(2) zona pellucida** → **(3) the egg's cell membrane**. `[E03.02]` **✔**
- Book: the zona protein **ZP3** binds the sperm and sets off the acrosome reaction. `[E03.02 · R05pa]` **⚑ → D-094 (a)** — Tier 1 (*mouse* eggs carrying *human* zona genes, tested with mouse and human sperm): eggs without **ZP2** — or with ZP2 missing its region 51–149 — bound **no** sperm and the females were sterile; that ZP2 domain is needed for sperm recognition in both species. The human zona has **four** proteins (ZP1–4), the mouse three.
- The acrosome's enzymes (acrosin, trypsin-like) let the sperm cut through the zona. `[E03.02]` **✔**
- Book: sperm and egg first stick **in part** through **integrins** on the egg and **disintegrins** on the sperm; then the membranes fuse at the back of the sperm head (the acrosome cap's membrane is already gone). `[E03.02 · R05pc, R05pm]` **⚑ → D-094 (b)** on the adhesion step — Tier 1 (*mouse*): eggs **lacking integrin α6β1** fertilize and bind sperm normally, while the egg tetraspanin CD9 is needed (R05pm); the essential pair is **IZUMO1** on the sperm and **JUNO** on the egg — Juno-null eggs do not fuse and the females are infertile; the pair binds in humans too (R05pc). · Fusion at the post-acrosomal head: **✔** book only
- In humans both the sperm head and tail enter the egg; the sperm's plasma membrane stays on the egg surface. `[E03.02]` **✔**
- The egg answers in three ways: **(1) cortical + zona reactions** block extra sperm (**polyspermy**); **(2)** it finishes **meiosis II** → a **second polar body** + the definitive oocyte, whose 23 chromosomes (22 + X) sit in the **female pronucleus**; **(3) metabolic activation**, probably started by a factor the sperm brings. `[E03.02 · R05pd]` **✔✔** on (3) (*human*, one case: a man whose sperm lack **PLCZ1** protein had fertilization failure; ICSI with assisted oocyte activation fertilized his partner's eggs, no pregnancy) · (1)–(2) **✔**
- Book: the cortical granules release **lysosomal enzymes** that change the zona. `[E03.02 · R05pb]` **⚑ → D-094 (c)** — Tier 1 (*mouse*): the granules carry **ovastacin**, an egg-specific protease (not a lysosomal enzyme) that **cuts ZP2**; without it ZP2 is not cut and sperm still bind fertilized eggs. ✚ *mouse*: after fusion, JUNO is shed fast from the egg membrane — a likely membrane block (R05pc), More only.
- The sperm nucleus swells into the **male pronucleus**; the tail detaches. The two pronuclei look alike, each **copies its DNA**, lose their envelopes, and the chromosomes line up for the first mitosis → **two-cell stage**. `[E03.02]` **✔**

### E03.02.03 Results of fertilization (A, core) · AN77.4
- **(1)** The **diploid number** is restored — half the chromosomes from each parent, a new combination. **(2)** **Chromosomal sex** is set: an X-bearing sperm → XX, a Y-bearing sperm → XY. **(3)** **Cleavage** starts. `[E03.02, E03.07]` **✔** (sex → link E16.02)
- ✚ The sperm's activating protein is **PLCζ** (gene *PLCZ1*); its loss is a known cause of male infertility with failed fertilization. `[R05pd]` **✚** More only, beyond Langman

### E03.03.01 Cleavage and morula (A, core) · AN78.1
- From the 2-cell stage the zygote divides by **mitosis** again and again; the cells (**blastomeres**) get **smaller with each division**, so the embryo does not grow. `[E03.03 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: 0.1–0.2 mm from CS1 through CS5) — refutes the map misconception "the embryo grows during cleavage".
- Timeline: 2 cells ~30 h, 4 cells ~40 h, 12–16 cells ~day 3, late morula ~day 4; all inside the **zona pellucida**, which disappears at the end of day 4. `[E03.03 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: CS2 = 2–16 cells, 1½–3 days)
- About day 3 the compacted embryo divides again into a **16-cell morula** ("mulberry"). `[E03.03]` **✔**

### E03.04.01 Blastocyst (A, core) · AN78.1
- About when the morula enters the uterine cavity, fluid seeps through the zona into the spaces **between the cells**; the spaces join into one cavity, the **blastocele** (blastocyst cavity) → the embryo is now a **blastocyst**. `[E03.04]` **✔** (note c: the book says the spaces "of the inner cell mass"; cards say between the cells)
- The inner cell mass, now the **embryoblast**, sits at **one pole**; the outer cells, the **trophoblast**, flatten into the blastocyst's wall. `[E03.04 · R05pe]` **✔✔** on two lineages, inner vs outer (*human* single-cell atlas, 1,529 cells from 88 embryos: trophectoderm, epiblast and primitive endoderm are set up as the blastocyst forms) · pole/wall layout ✔ book only
- The zona has gone, so implantation can begin. `[E03.04]` **✔**
- Book: the blastocyst is ~**4.5 days** old (Figs 3.10, 3.12; a section in Fig 3.10 has 107 cells). `[E03.04 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: CS3 free blastocyst ~4 days)

### E03.04.02 Implantation begins (A, core) · AN78.3
- In the human, the trophoblast at the embryo's end starts to wedge into the uterine lining **around day 6**. `[E03.04 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: CS4 = attaching blastocyst, 5–6 days; CS5 implanted, 7–12 days)
- Capture first, then invasion: **L-selectin** on the trophoblast binds sugar receptors on the uterine epithelium (like white cells being caught from the blood); then **integrins** on the trophoblast bind **laminin** (attachment) and **fibronectin** (migration). `[E03.04]` **✔**
- So by the end of week 1 the conceptus has passed morula and blastocyst and begun to implant — a two-way process between trophoblast and endometrium. `[E03.04]` **✔**
- Where: normally on the **anterior or posterior wall of the uterine body** (misconception 12; full line in §3g, E03.06.01). `[E03.06]` **✔**
- ✚ *Human* urine studies date implantation by the first **hCG**: in pregnancies that continued, 6–12 days after ovulation, **84 %** on days 8–10; the later it appears, the higher the early loss (13 % by day 9 → 82 % after day 11). `[R05ph]` **✚** More only — hCG appears only once the trophoblast is in and secreting, so it trails the day-6 start (note d)

### E03.05.01 Epiblast and hypoblast (A, core) · AN78.4
- Book: at the early blastocyst, under the influence of **FGFs**, embryoblast cells become **epiblast** or **hypoblast**. `[E03.05 · R05pf]` **⚑ → D-094 (d)** — Tier 1 (*human* blastocysts): epiblast (NANOG) and hypoblast (GATA4) cells do segregate, as in rodents, but **blocking FGF signalling does not stop human hypoblast forming** — unlike the mouse and rat.
- At first the two kinds are **scattered**; near implantation they sort out — epiblast **dorsally**, hypoblast **ventrally**, next to the blastocyst cavity. This sets the **dorsal–ventral** axis. `[E03.05]` **✔** (link E00.01.01)
- ✚ What each becomes (Ch 3 does not say; Ch 4–5 do): epiblast → the embryo proper; hypoblast → the anterior signalling centre and the yolk sac. `[R05pg]` **✚** (*human*) — cards take this from Ch 4–5 (X1.04)

## 6. Top student misconceptions (refute in cards and Checks — craft §3.4)
1. **"Ovulation is always on day 14."** It is midcycle *on average*; only ~3 in 10 women's fertile window sits inside days 10–17 `[E03.01 · R05ox, -02]` ✔✔.
2. **"Fertilization happens in the uterus."** In the ampulla of the uterine tube `[E03.02]` ✔.
3. **"The embryo grows during cleavage."** Cells get smaller; the conceptus stays ~0.1–0.2 mm all week `[E03.03 · R06]` ✔✔.
4. **"The first sperm to arrive fertilizes the egg."** Sperm need ~7 h of capacitation; racing gives no advantage `[E03.02]` ✔.
5. **"Only one sperm reaches the egg."** Hundreds reach the ampulla and several stick in the zona; one gets in `[E03.02]` ✔.
6. **"The egg finishes meiosis at ovulation."** It finishes meiosis II only after the sperm enters `[E03.02]` ✔ (link Ch 2).
7. **"The sperm only delivers DNA."** It brings the trigger that activates the egg (PLCζ, ✚) `[E03.02 · R05pd]` ✔✔.
8. **"Sex is decided later, when the gonads form."** Chromosomal sex is fixed at fertilization by the sperm `[E03.02]` ✔.
9. **"Sperm live only a few hours."** Several days — the fertile window is 6 days `[E03.02 · R05aq]` ✔✔.
10. **"The morula is a hollow ball."** The morula is solid; the cavity comes with the blastocyst `[E03.03, E03.04]` ✔.
11. **"The whole blastocyst becomes the baby."** Only the inner cell mass (embryoblast); the wall becomes trophoblast and placenta `[E03.04 · R05pe]` ✔✔.
12. **"Implantation happens in the tube."** Normally in the anterior or posterior wall of the uterine body; the tube is ectopic `[E03.06]` ✔ (link E04.04.04).
13. **"The corpus luteum is only a menstrual-cycle structure."** hCG keeps it working in early pregnancy `[E03.01]` ✔.
14. **"All of the endometrium is shed each month."** The basal layer stays and rebuilds `[E03.06]` ✔.
15. **"The head end is chosen at gastrulation."** It is set by the AVE near implantation, over a week before the streak `[E03.05 · R05pg]` ✔✔.
16. **"Most pregnancy loss is noticed."** Many losses happen before a woman knows she is pregnant `[E03.05 CC · R05pi]` ✔✔.
