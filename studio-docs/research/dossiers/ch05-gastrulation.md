## 2. ⚑ Watch item — notochord formation (E05.02.01)
- **Newer human data (R05, de Bree et al. 2018, 27 embryos CS8–15):** the notochordal process and plate form head-to-tail, but the **definitive notochord first appears in the middle of the embryo and extends both ways**; formation spans roughly CS8–CS12 (days 17–30).
