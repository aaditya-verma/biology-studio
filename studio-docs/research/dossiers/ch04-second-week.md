## 2. Tier A facts — cited and marked
### E04.00.01 Cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast (A, core) · AN78.2
- By day 8 the trophoblast over the inner cell mass has split into two layers: an inner **cytotrophoblast** of single-nucleus cells and an outer **syncytiotrophoblast**, a many-nucleus mass with no cell borders. `[E04.01 · R05pq]` **✔✔** on the two layers (*human* embryos in 3D culture: after implantation cytotrophoblast, syncytiotrophoblast and extravillous trophoblast all differentiate)
- Cells divide only in the cytotrophoblast; new cells move out into the syncytium and fuse into it, losing their membranes. `[E04.01]` **✔**
- The syncytiotrophoblast is the invading, eroding layer; the Summary calls the cytotrophoblast the actively dividing one. `[E04.05]` **✔**
- The syncytiotrophoblast makes the hormones of pregnancy, including **hCG**. `[E04.04 CC]` **✔** (Ch 8 tells the hormone story, `[E08]`)

### E04.01.01 Bilaminar disc (A, core) · AN78.4
- At day 8 the inner cell mass (embryoblast) is two layers: small cuboidal **hypoblast** cells facing the blastocyst cavity and tall columnar **epiblast** cells facing the new amniotic cavity. Together they make a flat **bilaminar disc**. `[E04.01 · R06, R05pq]` **✔✔** (R06: CS5 bilaminar disc; *human* 3D culture: disc forms as in vivo)
- The disc grows slowly compared with the trophoblast and is still only **0.1–0.2 mm** across at days 11–12. `[E04.03 · R06]` **✔✔**
- Embryos of the same age don't all develop at the same pace — differences show even this early. `[E04.00]` **✔** (context: R06 gives stage ages only as estimates, D-030) — refutes the misconception "every embryo hits each milestone on the same day".
- "Week of 2's" (Summary): two trophoblast layers, two disc layers, two layers of extraembryonic mesoderm, two cavities (amnion, yolk sac). `[E04.05]` **✔** (note h: a third, the chorionic cavity, also appears — cards keep the mnemonic but show three cavities)

### E04.01.02 Amniotic cavity (A) · AN78.4, AN80.1
- At day 8 a small cleft opens **inside the epiblast** and grows into the **amniotic cavity**. `[E04.01 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: the amniotic cavity begins forming in 5a)
- Epiblast cells next to the cytotrophoblast become **amnioblasts**; amnioblasts and the remaining epiblast together form the cavity's lining. `[E04.01 · R05pr, R05pq]` **✔✔** (*human* stem-cell model, markers checked in *macaque* embryos: amnion arises from epiblast as the embryo implants; *human* 3D culture: amnion distinct from epiblast) — refutes "the amnion comes from the trophoblast".
- ✚ The switch from epiblast to amnion is driven by **BMP** signalling, then the factor **TFAP2A**. `[R05pr]` **✚** More only, beyond Langman (*human stem-cell model*)

### E04.03.01 Uteroplacental circulation begins (A, threshold) · AN78.2, AN80.3
- Days 11–12: the syncytium reaches deeper and erodes the lining of congested, dilated maternal capillaries — **sinusoids**. The lacunae join them and **maternal blood enters the lacunar network**. `[E04.03 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: CS5b few blood cells; by CS5c the lacunar blood forms a ring ~1 mm across)
- Lacunae form at day 9 when vacuoles in the syncytium fuse — the **lacunar stage**; by days 11–12 they are a connected network, most marked at the embryonic pole. `[E04.02, E04.03 · R06]` **✔✔** (shared with E04.02.01)
- Book: as more sinusoids are eroded, maternal blood **begins to flow** through the trophoblast, setting up the **uteroplacental circulation** by the end of week 2 (Summary: a "primitive" one). `[E04.03, E04.05 · R05pn, R05po]` **⚑ → D-095 (a)** — Tier 1 (*human*, in situ): free **arterial** maternal flow into the placenta begins only at the **end of the first trimester** — intraplacental oxygen climbs from < 20 mmHg at 8 weeks' gestation to > 50 at 12 weeks as the uterine arteries open; until then uterine **gland secretions** feed the conceptus (histiotrophic nutrition) and the full intraplacental maternal circulation dates from the third month. *Clock:* "weeks' gestation" counts from the last period — 8–12 weeks' gestation ≈ weeks 6–10 after fertilization.
- Book: around day 13, extra blood flow into the lacunae can cause **bleeding** at the implantation site near day 28 of the cycle, which may be taken for a period and throw off the due date. `[E04.04 · R05pt]` **⚑ → D-095 (e)** — Tier 1 (*human* prospective cohort, 151 clinical pregnancies, daily diaries + urine hormones): 9 % bled in the first 8 weeks, lightly and around the expected period but **rarely on the implantation day**; the authors found **no support for implantation bleeding** and say such bleeding is unlikely to be mistaken for a period or to distort LMP-based dating.

### E04.04.03 Chorionic cavity and connecting stalk (A, core) · AN78.4, AN80.1, AN80.2
- Days 11–12: large spaces open in the extraembryonic mesoderm and merge into the **extraembryonic coelom = chorionic cavity**, which surrounds the yolk sac and amnion. `[E04.03 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: chorionic cavity distended again by CS5c — note j on R06's wording)
- The **connecting stalk** is the one bridge of mesoderm across the cavity, tying the disc to the trophoblast. `[E04.03, E04.04 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: stalk present in stage 6, sometimes not yet; vessel primordia in it by the end of 6b)
- Day 13: the chorionic cavity has grown large; the mesoderm coating the inner face of the shell is now called the **chorionic plate**. `[E04.04]` **✔**
- When blood vessels develop in it, the connecting stalk becomes the **umbilical cord**. `[E04.04 · R06]` **✔✔** (R06: vessel primordia in the stalk by late 6b; Ch 8 cord story, `[E08.05]`)
- Mesoderm on the cytotrophoblast and amnion = **extraembryonic somatic mesoderm**; on the yolk sac = **extraembryonic splanchnic mesoderm**. `[E04.03]` **✔** (shared with E04.03.02)

## 6. Top student misconceptions (refute in cards and Checks — craft §3.4)
1. **"The embryo is a big disc by week 2."** It is 0.1–0.2 mm — the trophoblast around it is far bigger `[E04.03 · R06]` ✔✔.
2. **"The amnion comes from the trophoblast."** It comes from the epiblast `[E04.01 · R05pr]` ✔✔.
3. **"There is only one yolk sac."** A primitive one (day 9) is replaced by a smaller secondary one (day 13) `[E04.02, E04.04 · R06]` ✔✔.
4. **"The yolk sac and the chorionic cavity are the same space."** The chorionic cavity is a new space in the extraembryonic mesoderm, around both amnion and yolk sac `[E04.03]` ✔.
5. **"Maternal blood pours through the placenta from week 2."** Some blood enters the lacunae early, but true arterial flow starts at the end of the first trimester (⚑ a) `[E04.03 · R05pn]`.
6. **"The decidua forms only because an embryo implants."** In humans it starts every cycle; implantation keeps it going (⚑ f) `[R05ps]`.
7. **"Syncytiotrophoblast cells divide."** Only the cytotrophoblast divides; its cells fuse into the syncytium `[E04.01]` ✔.
8. **"Ectopic = always in the tube."** ~95 %, but also the ovary or the abdomen `[E04.04 CC · R05pv]` ✔✔.
9. **"Placenta praevia is an ectopic pregnancy."** Praevia is inside the uterus, over the cervix `[E04.04 CC]` ✔.
10. **"A mole is a normal pregnancy with a growth."** A complete mole has no embryo and only the father's chromosomes `[E04.04 CC · R05qd]` ✔✔.
11. **"The connecting stalk disappears."** It becomes the umbilical cord `[E04.04 · R06]` ✔✔.
12. **"Every embryo hits each milestone on the same day."** Rates vary even now `[E04.00]` ✔.
13. **"Light bleeding around the first missed period (week 4 of pregnancy, gestational ≈ day 28 of the cycle) is the implantation bleed."** Tier 1 found no support for implantation bleeding (⚑ e) `[R05pt]`.
